What I Still Mean

Hey Ntomb'enhle,

I've been thinking about us lately not with regret, but with a lot of warmth. You were one of the best things that happened to me, and I don't want that to get lost just because things changed.

I remember the small stuff most: the way you laughed, the way you made ordinary days feel like something worth remembering. I still smile thinking about it.

This isn't me asking for anything back. I just wanted you to know that what we had mattered to me, and that I still hold it and you with a lot of care.

— always fondly
